Paste a Kinyarwanda news article and press File. The desk proposes one of the 14 KinNews sections, and when the score is under the cutoff chosen on development data it leaves the piece for an editor. The model is a character n-gram TF-IDF logistic regression that runs in your browser, so the text you paste is not sent anywhere.

Code, data, experiments, and limits: https://github.com/Keaane/Ikinyamakuru

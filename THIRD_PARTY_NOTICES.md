# Third-party notices

## KinNews (KINNEWS and KIRNEWS corpus)

The dataset is by Niyongabo, Qu, Kreutzer, and Huang (2020) and is published in the [KINNEWS-and-KIRNEWS-Corpus](https://github.com/Andrews2017/KINNEWS-and-KIRNEWS-Corpus) repository under the MIT licence. The release notes state that the copyright of the news articles belongs to the original news sources.

This project uses the raw KINNEWS train and test files. `data/raw/` holds deduplicated splits derived from them, and `results/` holds per-article predictions, for research and coursework. The original CSVs are not redistributed here.
